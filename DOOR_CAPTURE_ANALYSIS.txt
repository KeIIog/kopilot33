The same frames also appear on src/bus 130 in the log. That is treated as a forwarded/returned copy, not as a transmission bus. Candidate injection uses bus 0 only.

Supporting lock-state transitions were also observed on 0x411 (DOORS_SEATBELTS in the Hyundai CAN-FD DBC) and 0x414, confirming that the capture includes real lock/unlock state transitions.

IMPORTANT:
This evidence does NOT prove 0x3FF is an actuator command. It may be a request/event/feedback frame, and Panda safety or the vehicle can reject it. The candidate is intentionally disabled by default. Test only with ignition ON, Park, standstill, valid CAN, and openpilot disengaged. Never weaken Panda safety to force it through.
